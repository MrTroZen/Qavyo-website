A clearer way to finish the day.

A standardized, step-by-step closing flow prevents forgotten tasks and gives every manager a reliable structure.

Step 1 Service Ends

Dining room orders settle and open bar tabs are closed at POS.

Step 2 Review Sales

Check gross takings, discounts, comps, and refunds for accuracy.

Step 3 Record Expenses

Enter shift pay-outs, petty cash receipts, and emergency purchases.

Step 4 Count Cash

Perform a blind cash count of notes, coins, and register floats.

Step 5 Compare Figures

Match expected cash in drawer against the counted total.

Step 6 Review Difference

Identify and note any small cash variances or coin rounding.

Step 7 Close Day

Shift manager locks register, seals the till, and archives records.

Step 8 Review Reporting

P&L summary, channels, and manager reports update automatically.
