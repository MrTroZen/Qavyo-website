Human Control & Transparency

Qavyo can surface the change. You decide what happens next.

We believe restaurant operators make the best decisions when provided with clear, contextual facts. Qavyo does not run your business on autopilot.

What Qavyo Intelligence Does

  • Highlights significant changes: Surfaces deviations from rolling service and financial baselines.
  • Correlates context: Connects supplier cost changes to affected recipes and sales mix.
  • Points to source records: Gives you direct 1-click links into inventory, team, or till logs.

What You (The Operator) Control

  • Menu pricing: Only humans decide whether to update guest-facing prices.
  • Staffing decisions: Management reviews schedules, shifts, and team rosters personally.
  • Purchasing orders: Every supplier order requires explicit operator approval.
