Design system / 01

A calm foundation for connected operations.

This internal page verifies Qavyo’s shared type, colour, spacing, surfaces and controls. It is not the public homepage.

Primary action → Secondary action ↓

Typography

Strong, direct and easy to scan.

Qavyo connects the signals across a business and turns them into clearer next steps.

Body copy stays readable and measured. Supporting text uses a quieter tone without sacrificing contrast or dropping to impractical sizes.

Small text is reserved for genuinely secondary context, labels and metadata.
