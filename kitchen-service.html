The kitchen starts with the right order information.

Whether orders come from the counter POS, website, table QR code, or kiosk, every ticket is captured, validated, and structured before reaching the preparation line.

See what is new, what is being prepared and what is ready.

A clear visual display replaces lost paper chits with coordinated ticket management. Kitchen teams see active orders, item details, modifiers, and elapsed time at a glance.

  • Clear status columns
  • Order ticket timers
  • Modifier callouts
  • Preparation visibility
  • Direct pass notification
  • Reduced ticket confusion

Send each item to the station that needs it.

Route kitchen work to the relevant preparation area so each cook focuses only on what belongs on their line without sorting through unrelated tickets.

Know where every order is in preparation.

Track active orders as they move from received to preparing to ready. Understand which tickets are waiting, what is actively being cooked, and what is finished for the pass.

  • Real-time ticket progression
  • Precise prep timing records
  • Wait-time transparency
  • Server pass notification

See when something needs attention.

Surface orders or kitchen activity that may need attention during high-volume service before small delays become customer complaints.

  • Target preparation thresholds
  • Station queue volume alerts
  • Delayed item callouts
  • Clear operational signals

What the kitchen prepares also affects what the restaurant has left.

Every dish prepared uses ingredients. Connecting kitchen tickets with recipes and inventory provides visibility over remaining ingredient stock and depletion without manual counts.
