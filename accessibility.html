Accessibility at Qavyo.

We aim to make the public website understandable, navigable and usable across different devices and ways of interacting.

Statement date: 21 October 2026

Accessibility is checked as pages are built and again during the final launch audit.

What the website supports

  • Semantic headings and landmarks.
  • Keyboard navigation and visible focus indicators.
  • Text labels for statuses rather than colour alone.
  • Responsive layouts and touch-friendly controls.
  • Reduced-motion preferences where motion exists.
  • Form labels, instructions and inline validation messages.

Known limitations

The website remains under active development. Some legacy scaffold pages may still require a complete accessibility and responsive review before they are considered final.

Report a problem

If you encounter an accessibility barrier, use the Contact page. Include the page address, what you were trying to do and the assistive technology or device involved, if you are comfortable sharing it.

Review commitment

This statement should be updated when the production domain, connected services and final site audit are complete.
