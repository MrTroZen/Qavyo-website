Different ordering experiences for different moments.

Each ordering channel serves a distinct customer scenario, but all feed into the same restaurant workflow.

Customer scenario Remote orders via website

Customer orders from their home, office, or phone via the restaurant's website.

Best suited for Advance orders & takeaway

Guests scheduling orders ahead of time, planning pickup, or ordering delivery.

Operational handoff Direct queue entry

Order arrives with scheduled fulfillment time directly into the kitchen queue.

Customer scenario At-table smartphone ordering

Customer scans a printed code at their dining table and browses on their device.

Best suited for Dine-in tables & terraces

Busy service hours, casual dining, outdoor seating, and quick drink reorders.

Operational handoff Table-tagged kitchen ticket

Order routes immediately to kitchen stations with table number attached.

Customer scenario Dedicated touchscreen station

Customer uses an in-restaurant self-service terminal to place their order.

Best suited for Counter & quick-service

Handling peak arrival rushes, high footfall, and self-directed meal customization.

Operational handoff Order ticket generation

Prints or displays customer order ticket and notifies preparation stations.

Customer scenario Mobile-optimised web browsing

Lightweight touch interface designed for quick ordering on smartphones.

Best suited for On-the-go & repeat guests

Commuters, rapid lunchtime reorders, and customers ordering while en route.

Operational handoff Live kitchen synchronization

Feeds directly into active kitchen display alongside POS and QR orders.
