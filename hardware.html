Different jobs need different screens. The software stays connected.

In a restaurant, hardware is an access point to your software. Whether operating a counter touchscreen, a kitchen bump bar, a customer-facing screen, or a self-service kiosk, every device shares one live operational record.

Hardware Strategy

Choose the setup around how the restaurant operates.

Hardware follows workflow. You do not need to buy an entire catalog of devices; select only the screens that serve a concrete role in your service.

Scale device deployment as your service evolves:

Start with a single counter POS station and payment reader. Add kitchen displays when paper tickets become a service bottleneck, or introduce customer-facing kiosks as customer volume grows. Qavyo connects every added device into your existing account seamlessly.
