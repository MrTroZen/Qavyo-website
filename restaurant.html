Qavyo Intelligence

Know what needs your attention.

Qavyo Intelligence connects restaurant signals, explains why they matter, and recommends a next step. You decide whether anything changes.

  1. Watch
  2. Detect
  3. Explain
  4. Recommend
  5. Act
  6. Measure
  7. Learn
Observation

Food cost is rising faster than sales.

A connected view brings the relevant sales, recipe, stock, and purchasing signals together for review.

  1. Reason

    Recent purchasing and ingredient usage show where the change is coming from.

  2. Prediction

    If the pattern continues, pressure on margin is likely to increase.

  3. Recommendation

    Review affected ingredients, portions, suppliers, and menu pricing.

  4. Expected impact

    Compare the available response before approving a change.

  5. Action

    An authorised operator chooses what to apply.

Human control: Qavyo Intelligence does not change prices, orders, recipes, stock, schedules, or payroll without operator approval.
