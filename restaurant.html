What do I get during the first 30 days?

You get eligible restaurant software and Full Qavyo Intelligence for 30 days. Afterward, choose the plan you want to continue with.

Are hardware and payments included in the software price?

No. Dedicated hardware and payment processing are separate from the restaurant software subscription.

Can I use devices I already own?

Supported Qavyo experiences may run on compatible phones, tablets, and equipment. Compatibility depends on the intended setup and must be confirmed.

Where are smaller features such as floor plan or recipes?

Smaller features live inside their closest major workflow. Floor plan belongs with Restaurant POS; recipes belong with Inventory & Food Cost.

Does Qavyo Intelligence make changes automatically?

No. It can surface signals, context, and recommendations, but an authorised operator controls any resulting change.
