Needs Attention Cost Variance & Margin Protection
Operational Data Verified
● Observation

Food cost is rising faster than sales across dinner service for the past 5 days.

↳ Reason

Recent supplier invoice recorded a +18.4% cost increase on beef tenderloin cuts. No corresponding menu price update was applied, and dinner volume remained at 48 covers/day.

↳ Prediction

Overall kitchen gross profit margin will compress by 3.2% (approx. £1,420 monthly variance) over the next 14 days if portioning and menu pricing remain unchanged.

↳ Recommendation

Option A: Update tenderloin main course price from £28.50 to £30.00 (+£1.50).
Option B: Review standard recipe batch portioning from 240g to 225g and request secondary quote from backup approved butcher supplier.

↳ Expected Impact

Restores dish gross margin to target 68.5% and preserves £1,420 in expected monthly gross profit without diminishing guest volume.

✓ Operator Action

Operator approves Option A. Updated menu pricing syncs immediately to all POS terminals and online menus. Recommendations support restaurant operators. Qavyo Intelligence never modifies pricing, recipes, stock orders, staff shifts, or payroll autonomously without explicit human approval.

Operational Signals Across Live Service

INVENTORY

Stock movement is outside its recent pattern.

Tracks abnormal kitchen ingredient depletion against live order volume to flag unticketed prep or recipe over-portioning early.

PURCHASING

Ingredient cost movement deserves attention.

Compares new supplier invoice lines against previous purchase orders to highlight cost variances before they erode dish margins.

SALES

Lunch sales are running below their recent pattern.

Identifies shifts in midday cover volume and takeout velocity, allowing managers to adjust staffing or promote targeted lunch offerings.

MULTI-LOCATION

One location deserves review.

Highlights unit-level variances in food cost % or ticket turn times across venues so operators can share best practices across sites.
