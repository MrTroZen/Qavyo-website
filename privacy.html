Privacy notice.

This notice explains the current public website prototype and the information its demonstration forms handle.

Last updated: 21 October 2026

This draft should receive legal review before production use.

Current website behaviour

The public prototype presents Qavyo products and services. Its demonstration forms validate information in your browser but do not transmit the entered information to Qavyo because no submission service is connected.

Information a production form may request

Contact and sales forms may request a name, work email, business name, business type and the message you choose to provide. A production implementation should collect only what is needed to answer the enquiry.

How information would be used

  • Respond to a contact, sales, support or access request.
  • Understand the relevant product or operational context.
  • Maintain security and prevent misuse where a live service is connected.

Cookies and analytics

No analytics or advertising claim is made by this prototype. Any future cookie, analytics or consent implementation must be documented here before launch.

Your choices

Do not enter sensitive personal information, passwords or payment-card information in public forms. For privacy questions, use the Contact page.
