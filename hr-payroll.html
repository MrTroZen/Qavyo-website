Build the week around the people running the restaurant.

Create visual weekly schedules tailored to service demand. Assign shifts across floor, bar, and kitchen stations without complicated spreadsheets.

Compare the shift that was planned with the time that was worked.

Staff clock in and out on the POS terminal or designated store tablet. Managers get immediate side-by-side visibility between scheduled rota hours and actual worked times.

Easily verify early arrivals, overtime, and lateness before approving timesheets for the pay period.

Turn worked time into a clearer payroll workflow.

Approved timesheets flow directly into payroll calculation summaries. Export or review accurate gross pay figures based on contracted hourly rates, approved overtime, and recorded tip shares.

Qavyo organizes and calculates verified restaurant pay inputs so you can run payroll cleanly without manual calculations.

Keep tips visible alongside staff pay.

Whether tips are pooled by shift or allocated by hours worked, Qavyo keeps recorded card and digital gratuities transparent so team members and managers see clear distributions.

See what labour cost looked like against the day you ran.

Understand the commercial relationship between staff cost and sales volume. Evaluate daily labour cost percentages across front-of-house, kitchen, and management teams.

Review team performance with operational context.

Evaluate service periods using real operational evidence — attendance reliability, kitchen ticket preparation times, and scheduled versus worked hours.

Qavyo does not use automated scoring or artificial rankings. Operators review real service numbers to make informed management decisions.

Give staff access to the information they need away from the manager's screen.

With the Qavyo Staff App, team members can check their upcoming rotas, review assigned service stations, track worked hours, and view recent tip shares directly from their mobile devices.

Reduces rota enquiries and keeps team communication clear without manager back-and-forth.

Manage teams across more than one restaurant.

For multi-unit operators, coordinate staff across separate restaurant locations from a single dashboard. Standardize permissions, manage location-specific rotas, and monitor group labour costs.
