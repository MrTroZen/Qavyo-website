Order to Payment

Payment is the last part of the order, not a separate system.

In a restaurant, payment is not an isolated transaction. It begins at the table or counter, settles the guest check, updates kitchen and inventory statuses, and flows directly into end-of-day balances and managerial reporting.

05

Day Close & Reporting

Audit & Visibility

Keep payment status connected to the order.

Every ticket retains its explicit payment and operational status so front-of-house and management never guess whether a check is open, settled, or adjusted.

Status Term Operational Meaning POS & Floor Effect Reporting & Close Impact
Paid Transaction successfully authorized and recorded for full total. Table ready to be cleared or reset in floor plan. Included in shift sales totals and expected drawer/tender reconciliation.
