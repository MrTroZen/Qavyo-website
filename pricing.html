Pricing FAQ

Before you choose.

Clear answers about the trial, plans and separate costs.

What do I get during the first 30 days?

The full eligible Qavyo software platform and Full Qavyo Intelligence are unlocked for the 30-day experience.

What happens after 30 days?

You choose Starter at £29/month, Growth at £79/month, or Business at £149/month.

Is hardware included in the software price?

No. Hardware is separate from the monthly software subscription.

Do I need to buy new hardware?

Not necessarily. Supported compatible devices may be usable, while dedicated hardware is available separately. Confirm your setup with Qavyo.

Are payment-processing fees included?

No. Payment-processing fees and provider terms are separate.

What's the difference between Intelligence levels?

Starter includes Essential Intelligence, Growth includes Full Intelligence, and Business includes Full plus Multi-location Intelligence. Explore Qavyo Intelligence.

Can I get help choosing a plan?

Yes. Talk to the Qavyo sales team about your operation and setup.
