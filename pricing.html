Questions before you start?

Is £29 really all I pay for Starter?

Starter is £29/month for the Qavyo software included in that plan. Hardware, payment processing and third-party services are separate where applicable.

Do I need to buy a new POS terminal?

No. Qavyo is designed to run on compatible tablets, phones and computers you already own. Add extra hardware only when you need it.

What happens during the first 30 days?

You get access to the complete Qavyo platform, so you can use the features before choosing Starter, Growth or Business.

What happens if I choose Starter after using Growth features?

After your full-access period, features outside Starter are no longer available unless you choose a plan that includes them. Your core Starter tools continue with your Starter subscription.

What does 50% off the first year mean?

Your first annual year is 50% off the equivalent monthly price and is paid upfront. Normal plan pricing applies after your first year.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. You can move to a different Qavyo plan as your restaurant needs change.

Are payment processing fees included?

No. Payment processing is separate from the Qavyo software subscription.

Can Qavyo work with my existing hardware?

Qavyo supports compatible devices and equipment. Exact compatibility depends on your setup.

Is there a long-term software contract?

No. Qavyo software subscriptions do not require a long-term contract.
