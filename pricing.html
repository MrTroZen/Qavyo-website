Questions before you start?

What happens after the first 30 days?

You choose Starter, Growth or Business and continue with the plan that fits your restaurant.

Do I get the full platform during the first 30 days?

Yes. New customers can explore the complete Qavyo platform before choosing a plan.

What does 50% off the first year mean?

Annual customers receive 50% off their first year. The full annual amount is paid upfront and normal annual pricing applies after the first year.

Can I use my existing tablet, phone or computer?

Yes, on compatible devices. Qavyo is designed so restaurants can start without replacing their whole setup.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Customers can move to a different plan as their restaurant needs change.

Are hardware costs included?

No. Hardware is separate. You can start with compatible devices you already own and add hardware when needed.

Are payment processing fees included?

No. Payment processing is separate from the Qavyo software subscription.

Is there a long-term software contract?

No. Qavyo software subscriptions have no long-term contracts.
