Restaurant Mental Model

A delivery order still has to move through the restaurant.

Delivery is not isolated from daily restaurant service. It begins with digital ordering, enters kitchen stations for hot preparation, and finishes at a verified handoff point.

STEP 01 Online Order

Customer selects delivery fulfilment on your restaurant web ordering experience.

STEP 02 Qavyo Order

Order enters the central queue clearly marked with off-premise fulfilment tags.

STEP 03 Kitchen

Tickets route to appropriate station screens (Grill, Fry, Pack) with clear modifiers.

STEP 04 Ready

Chef marks dishes ready; packed into bag and moved to the designated collection counter.

STEP 05 Supported Handoff

Order handed over, marked dispatched or collected, and committed to customer history.
